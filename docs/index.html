Verza

All of YouTube Music. No ads. On your computer and your phone.

Get it See it first

Free. No sign-up, nothing tracked. Windows, Linux and Android.

The scene moves

3D covers

Album covers with a person in front of a scene now move in 3D.

Move your pointer over Now Playing, or tilt your phone, and look around.

Flat artwork stays flat. Turn it off anytime in Settings. On computers, Windows only for now.

Colour from the cover

Now Playing

The album cover flows behind every song, seen through ribbed glass.

Each song picks its own accent colour from its cover.

This page does it too. Scroll and watch the player at the bottom.

Phone and computer

Flick it over

Flick your phone toward your computer and the song moves there, right where you left off.

Flick it back to take the music with you.

The queue comes too

Up to 500 songs move with it.

Almost instant

The song starts on your computer in about 150 milliseconds.

Verza Connect

Pair once with a six-digit code. Then control your computer from your phone, and keep playlists and likes in sync.

Stays on your Wi-Fi

Your phone and computer talk to each other directly. Nothing goes through a server.

Downloads

Lossless downloads

Music / Coldplay / Parachutes / Coldplay - Yellow.flac

Download songs as FLAC, as good as the original recording. Turn on Download in lossless and Verza does the rest.

It finds the best copy shared on Soulseek and skips the fakes.

Every file is tagged and filed in your Music folder by artist and album, ready for any player.

Already have the song? Verza plays your file instead of streaming it.

Home and Your Sound

Made from what you play

Home

Recently played, on repeat, forgotten favourites and mixes based on what you love.

Discovery radio

New songs in the genre you love. Never ones you've already heard.

Your Sound

See what, when and how much you listen, and get a radio of your genre.

Your account, if you want

Sign in to bring your likes, playlists and Home. Or skip it, and everything else still works.

Verza for Android

In your pocket

The same Verza, on Android. 3D covers move as you tilt your phone.

Play in Android Auto and from your lock screen, with home-screen widgets and a quick-settings tile.
