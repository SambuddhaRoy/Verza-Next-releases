A free YouTube Music player for Windows

What Verza Next does

  • Offline downloadsSave a song, an album or an entire playlist to your PC and play it with no internet connection.
  • Time-synced lyricsKaraoke-style lyrics that light up line by line, in time with the track.
  • 10-band equalizerPer-band gain with a bass shelf, plus crossfade, playback speed and output-device selection.
  • Discovery radioA radio that stays in the genre you started from but only plays songs you have never heard.
  • Your SoundPrivate listening insights — top artists, when you listen, and your comfort songs — computed on your device.
  • Import your libraryBring playlists over from Spotify, Apple Music, Deezer, TIDAL, Soundiiz or a JSON export.
  • Liquid-glass interfaceReal Windows 11 acrylic, six accent palettes plus a cover-adaptive theme, light and dark.
  • Desktop-nativeSystem tray, media keys, global hotkeys, launch at startup, sleep timer and notifications.

How to install Verza Next on Windows

  1. Download the installer (VerzaNext_x.y.z_x64-setup.exe).
  2. Run it. Windows SmartScreen may warn you because the installer is not code-signed — choose More info → Run anyway.
  3. Open Verza Next and either sign in for your library, or skip it — full-length playback works either way.

Verza Next updates itself: it checks on launch and installs new versions on restart.

Frequently asked questions

Is Verza Next free?

Yes — completely free, with no ads, no tracking and no subscription. It is a personal project shared for personal use.

Do I need a YouTube account?

No. Search, full-length playback, radio, lyrics and downloads all work signed out. Signing in only adds your personalised home feed and your YouTube Music library.

Can I download music for offline listening?

Yes. Download a song, an album or a whole playlist, and it plays with no connection.

Does it work on Windows 11?

Yes — Windows 10 and 11 (64-bit), with native Windows 11 acrylic translucency.

Why does Windows show a SmartScreen warning?

The installer is not code-signed (certificates cost hundreds a year), so Windows warns about any unrecognised installer. Choose More info → Run anyway.

Is there a macOS or Linux version?

Not yet. Windows 10 and 11 today.
